## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a sanctuary-inspired, comforting dark interface tailored for animal welfare, veterinary care, and pet adoption management. Rather than clinical detachment or sterile hospital whites, it communicates safety, devotion, and tactile warmth. The emotional landscape mirrors stepping out of the cold into a softly lit haven: grounded, empathetic, and organized.

The aesthetic fuses modern warm minimalism with subtle tactile depth. Deep earthen charcoal surfaces are infused with amber undertones to avoid the stark, eye-fatiguing sterility of pure black. Luminous honey and amber accents glow against these dark backdrops, evoking golden hour sunbeams, coats of fur, and unconditional companionship. Interfaces prioritize immediate legibility under stressful veterinary situations while preserving an approachable, heart-centered atmosphere for prospective pet parents.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by rich, warm-cast dark tones rather than synthetic neutral grays.

- **Background & Canvas:**
  - Base Canvas (`#12100e`): The deepest obsidian-amber shade, setting a peaceful resting state.
  - Surface Tier 1 (`#1c1815`): Container surface for foundational grouping and structural zones.
  - Surface Tier 2 (`#26211c`): Elevated cards, modals, and interactive tiles.
  - Surface Tier 3 (`#362f27`): Hover states, active highlights, and floating popovers.
  - Subtle Border (`#443a30`): Low-contrast borders framing interactive boundaries.

- **Warm Glow Primaries:**
  - Core Brand Amber (`#f59e0b`): The vibrant pulse of the system used for key calls-to-action, primary tags, and focal indicators.
  - Honey Accent (`#fbbf24`): Illuminating hover fills and active states.
  - Deep Caramel Amber (`#d97706`): Pressed states, interactive focus rings, and high-emphasis contours.

- **Text & Reading Surfaces:**
  - Primary Cream (`#fefce8`): Gentle, non-glare cream for headlines and primary body text.
  - Secondary Cream (`#fde68a`): Warm, soft amber-cream for subtext, captions, and meta badges.
  - Muted Amber (`#a89984`): Tertiary text, disabled states, and inactive icons.

- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - Clinical Safe / Positive (`#10b981` / `#059669`): Muted sage and warm emerald denoting stable vitals, cleared vaccinations, and adoption approvals.
  - Cautionary / Warning (`#e07a5f`): Soft warm terracotta for allergy warnings, dietary restrictions, and pending review flags.
  - Critical / Emergency (`#ef4444`): Unmistakable critical red for triage emergencies and severe flags.

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its rounded terminals and open apertures bring organic softness, natural friendliness, and modern balance without sacrificing typographic clarity during critical clinical tasks.

- **Hierarchy & Contrast:** Headlines feature tighter negative letter spacing and bold weights to ensure strong semantic distinction against dark containers. Body copy remains set at regular (400) weight with relaxed line-heights to support fatigue-free reading during long shifts.
- **Micro-Labels & Clinical Tags:** Labels (`label-md` and `label-sm`) employ slight letter-spacing expansions and semi-bold/bold weights to guarantee at-a-glance recognition on medication tags, kennel badges, and vitals indicators.
- **Responsive Handling:** Display and level-1 headline styles transition to tailored mobile scales below the 768px breakpoint to avoid awkward line wraps.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture underpins the interface, adapting across three core viewport tiers:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at `1440px` max-width to maintain comfortable scan lines across patient rosters and medical logs.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Secondary clinical panes collapse into drawer systems.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Grid items stack vertically into unified operational flows.

Spacing units follow a strict 4px/8px rhythm. Compact spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) binds interrelated patient metrics, while open spacing (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) isolates independent clinical workflows and adoption profile modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this sanctuary aesthetic is built through **tonal layering** and **warm, low-opacity ambient glows** rather than harsh directional dropshadows.

- **Surface Layering:** Elements elevate by stepping up through the amber-charcoal spectrum: `#12100e` (Canvas) → `#1c1815` (Containers) → `#26211c` (Cards) → `#362f27` (Floating Panels).
- **Amber-Tinted Ambient Shadows:** Elevated layers carry an ultra-diffuse shadow tinted with warm umber (`rgba(18, 16, 14, 0.75)` mixed with `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.04)`), preventing muddy black halos and mimicking gentle warm backlighting.
- **Luminescent Highlights:** Floating overlays, active popovers, and triage flyouts use a 1px border stroke (`#443a30`) combined with an inner border highlight (`rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.06)`) on the top edge to reflect soft downlighting.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** to evoke a welcoming, companionable posture while retaining clinical reliability. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and badges utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Animal profile cards, medication logs, and modal overlays step up to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Primary hero adoption features and floating bottom sheets use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Circular pills are reserved strictly for status chips, species tags, and patient avatar rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#f59e0b`, text `#12100e` (extra-bold), border `none`. Hover shifts to `#fbbf24` with a subtle glow (`0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`). Active scales to 0.98 with `#d97706`.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Background `#26211c`, text `#fefce8`, 1px border `#443a30`. Hover shifts background to `#362f27` and border to `#f59e0b`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#fde68a`. Hover background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Pill-shaped with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding.
  - *Adoptable / Healthy:* Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#6ee7b7`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
  - *Under Observation / Caution:* Background `rgba(224, 122, 95, 0.12)`, text `#fca5a5`, border `1px solid rgba(224, 122, 95, 0.3)`.
  - *Intake / In Care:* Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#fbbf24`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.

### Cards & Patient Tiles
- Constructed on `#26211c` with a `1px` border of `#362f27` and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Padding uses `space-md` (16px) for compact lists and `space-lg` (24px) for full profile viewports.
- Feature images (pet photos) apply subtle corner rounding matching the card's inner radius.

### Input Fields
- Background `#1c1815`, border `1px solid #443a30`, text `#fefce8`, placeholder `#a89984`.
- Focus state switches border to `#f59e0b` accompanied by a soft halo ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.25rem`. Background `#1c1815`, border `1.5px solid #443a30`.
- Checked state fills with `#f59e0b`, displaying a deep dark `#12100e` check icon.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Vitals Telemetry Strip:** A low-profile horizontal bar featuring dark ember backing (`#1c1815`), displaying pulse, weight, and temperature metrics paired with subtle green/terracotta delta indicators.
- **Kennel / Pen Allocation Indicator:** A partitioned mini-grid using warm cream labels and glowing status dots to rapidly reflect shelter capacity.
